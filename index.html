Welcome to Tarfex's Bridge Battle

Inspired by the Ford Mustang Mach-E

Game Instructions

Objective: Connect your side of the board to the opposite side using your color tiles.

  • Player 1 (Blue): connect top to bottom
  • Player 2 (Red): connect left to right

Gameplay:

  • Click on an empty square to place your tile.
  • The first player to connect their goal sides wins.
  • Reset anytime using the Reset button.
  • Background music can be muted using the Mute button.
  • Play against a friend or challenge the computer at Easy, Medium, or Hard.
