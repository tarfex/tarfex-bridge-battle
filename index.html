Welcome to Tarfex's Bridge Battle

Inspired by the Ford Mustang Mach-E

Connect your side of the board to the opposite side.

  • Player 1 (Blue): connect top to bottom
  • Player 2 (Red): connect left to right

Square board: tiles connect via 4 flat sides.

Hex board: hexagons connect via 6 sides. Draws are impossible!

  • Tap an empty cell to place your tile.
  • First to bridge wins. The path lights up green.
