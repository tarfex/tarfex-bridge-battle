Game Instructions

Welcome to Tarfex's Bridge Battle! Connect your color from one side of the board to the other:

  • Player 1 (Blue): Top to Bottom
  • Player 2 (Red): Left to Right

Take turns placing your tiles. The first player to create a connected path across their goal wins.

If playing vs Computer, select a difficulty level (1-10) where 1 is easiest and 10 is hardest.

You can reset the game at any time using the Reset button.

Use the Mute button to toggle background music.
