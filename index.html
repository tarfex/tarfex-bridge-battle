P1: Top↔Bottom

Goal: Be the first to build a connected bridge across the board.

  • Player 1 (Blue): connect the top edge to the bottom edge.
  • Player 2 (Red): connect the left edge to the right edge.

Square board: tiles connect via shared flat sides (4 neighbours).

Hex board: each hexagon connects to up to 6 neighbours. The rhombus shape and offset rows are classic Hex — two paths cannot both be blocked simultaneously.

  • Tap/click an empty cell to place your tile.
  • The winning path lights up green.
  • The game cannot end in a draw.
