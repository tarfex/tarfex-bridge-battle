Connect your side of the board to the opposite side using your colour tiles.

  • Player 1 (Blue): connect top to bottom
  • Player 2 (Red): connect left to right

Rules:

  • Tap an empty square to place your tile.
  • Tiles only connect via flat sides — diagonals do not count.
  • First to complete a bridge wins. The winning path lights up green.
  • If neither player can possibly win, the game ends in a Draw.
  • Reset anytime. Toggle music with the Mute button.
